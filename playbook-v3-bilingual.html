The red lines

These apply everywhere, every time — comments, DMs, captions, stories.

  • No claims about results. Never treat / cure / heal / fix your pain. You talk about position, angle, force, and how someone works.

    不主動談課程、不主動報價——除非對方自己先問。

  • No patient testimonials. Don't quote clients as proof.

    不用客人當見證。不要引用客人的話來當證明。

  • Your own hair loss is a story, never evidence. You may say: you lost hair in patches during your worst stretch of overwork, and it grew back after you changed how you lived and worked. That's the whole story, and it's yours to tell. You may never say, or imply, that acupuncture, massage, or anything you teach treated it, cured it, or made it grow back — and never that it will do the same for anyone else.

Timing and volume — when to sit down and reply · 時段與訊息量

You are not on call. Nothing here has to be answered quickly.

You sit down with the phone twice a day, and each sitting is about ten minutes:

  • Once after your last client of the day.

That's it. Ten minutes is how long the sitting lasts, not how fast you have to reply. Don't pick the phone up between clients, and don't answer the moment a notification comes in.

The only standard is 24 hours. Every comment and every DM gets an answer within a day. Anything that arrives after your evening sitting is tomorrow morning's job — that is normal and it costs you nothing.
