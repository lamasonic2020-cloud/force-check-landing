The last line about replying “stop” stays on every email — Canadian anti-spam law requires a way out and a way to identify you.

The red lines

These apply everywhere, every time.

  • No claims about results. Never treat / cure / heal / fix your pain. You talk about position, angle, force, and how someone works.
  • No diagnosing. If someone describes symptoms, use the line in the first toggle below.
  • No course talk and no prices — unless they ask first.
  • No patient testimonials. Don't quote clients as proof.
  • The hair loss story is only ever “it came back once I changed how I lived and worked.” Never that anything cured it.
  • Don't argue. One round, then stop.

Everything else

Open these only when you need them.

Other kinds of comments — questions, pushback, symptoms, bots

Reply to every comment, same day, one line each. The detail belongs in the DM.

They asked a question — one real answer, then invite the keyword.

Especially deep tissue — that's where thumbs get wrecked. Comment EFFORTLESS and I'll send you the check.
Example

They're pushing back (“meridians aren't science”) — answer once, never twice, don't delete. These comments push reach.

Pushback
Fair enough. I don't teach it as theory — I teach where the hands go, the angle, and how to load your body weight. Call it what you like; the mechanics are the same.

They describe symptoms and want a verdict

Symptoms
That's more than I can answer in a comment. If it's lasted over two weeks, please get it checked by a doctor or physio.

Friends and colleagues — answer like a human. Don't push the keyword at everyone.
Ads and bots — ignore and move on.

Stop rules. No reply, don't chase. Two turns maximum. No third message.

Someone DMs you without commenting

Treat it like a conversation with a colleague. No link, no email ask.

Opener
Hey, thanks for reaching out! Are you an RMT too? What's your work week like?

If they ask for the material outright, send them the step 1 DM. Don't make them go and comment.

Timing and volume

Two fixed slots a day — ten minutes before you start, ten minutes after you finish. It doesn't have to be instant.

Every comment and DM answered within 24 hours.

More than 20 new DMs in a day: do the keyword ones first, the rest tomorrow. A burst of DMs to people who don't follow you gets the account limited.

On the day a Reel goes out, be back in the comments within an hour. The first hour is worth the most.
